# Contributing criticism, evidence and prior art

This project welcomes criticism more than affirmation. The most useful contributions identify existing work, expose an unsupported assumption, narrow an over-broad claim, propose a falsifiable test or show how the framework fails under real conditions.

## Suggested contribution types

- **Prior-art correction:** an existing framework, term, institution or research tradition that already performs the claimed function.
- **Source correction:** a citation that does not support the wording, a missing source or a stronger primary source.
- **Counterargument or counterexample:** a condition under which a proposition fails or creates harm.
- **Claim-status correction:** language that treats a synthesis, hypothesis or unvalidated proposal as more established than it is.
- **Accessibility or participation gap:** a population, condition or practical barrier missing from the framework.
- **Pilot or evaluation suggestion:** a bounded way to test a specific assumption without presuming the framework is valid.

Please use the repository's Issues area and include a source link where one is relevant. Keep separate claims in separate issues where practical.

## Boundaries

- Do not post private conversations, personal case material or identifying third-party information.
- Do not treat lived experience as invalid merely because it is not generalizable; identify what kind of evidence it is and what it can support.
- Do not treat AI-generated critique, internal testing or editorial agreement as independent validation.
- Criticism may support revision, rejection, narrower framing or incorporation into established prior art. Agreement with the framework is not a contribution requirement.

Participation does not imply endorsement by Simon Delaile, and acknowledgement of a contribution does not automatically create co-authorship of the framework.
