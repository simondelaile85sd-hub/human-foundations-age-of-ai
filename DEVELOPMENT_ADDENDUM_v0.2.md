# Human Foundations — Development Addendum v0.2 (14 October 2026)

**Status:** Public working addendum for critique; not an empirically validated revision. This supplements, and does not silently replace, [v0.1](HUMAN_FOUNDATIONS_v0.1.md). The original release remains intact.

## 1. Constraint-aware agency (project synthesis / hypothesis)
The existing structural / usable / experienced agency distinction needs an explicit **feasible-choice test**. A formally available option may be unusable because of money, time, housing, safety, transport, caregiving, disability access, exhaustion, administrative friction, or uncertainty. Evaluation should compare *actually accessible* options, including declining or doing nothing, rather than an idealised menu of choices.

**Test question:** When an intervention advertises more choices, does it expand choices that people can realistically exercise under their conditions? Do people retain the ability to refuse without penalty?

## 2. Cognitive resources as an access condition (hypothesis)
Attention, time to reflect, sleep, information-processing burden, and ability to recover from overload may constrain usable agency. These conditions should not be inferred as stable traits or diagnoses. A support system may increase nominal opportunity while consuming the very cognitive resources required to use it.

**Test question:** Does a proposed service reduce navigation and decision costs without covertly substituting institutional or AI judgement for the person's own?

## 3. Interaction literacy as a human foundation (unvalidated educational proposal)
Critical AI literacy includes checking an AI's assumptions, the user's assumptions, and the blind spots shared by both. A practical reciprocal prompt is:

> What assumptions did I make? What assumptions did the AI make? What did we both miss?

This is a reflective practice, not a validated measurement instrument. It should include contesting fluent but unsupported answers, distinguishing evidence from interpretation, maintaining narrative authority over one's own experience, and recognising the limits of AI continuity and self-description. AI assistance should remain correctable and should not be treated as independent validation.

## 4. Interpretation discipline (methodological proposal)
Keep separate: **observation → possible interpretation(s) → hypothesis → test → revision**. Non-use, delay, refusal, absence, or silence cannot by themselves establish motivation, capacity, consent, or wellbeing. Record competing explanations and disconfirming evidence; do not turn a plausible narrative into a factual finding.

## Scope and prior-art boundary
These additions develop existing themes in v0.1 rather than claim to invent agency, cognitive accessibility, critical literacy, or evidence discipline. Relevant capability, accessibility, human-factors, education, and human–AI interaction literatures require targeted external review. No new empirical findings are claimed.

**Next review:** Check for duplication with existing frameworks; identify observable indicators and potential harms; invite criticism from people facing materially different constraints. This addendum does not change the separate status, rights, or pilot gates of Curiosity Commons.
